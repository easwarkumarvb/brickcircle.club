Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

BrickCircle is a community for adults to catalogue complete LEGO® sets, find local reciprocal matches, and arrange temporary exchanges. This notice describes the personal information BrickCircle handles and the choices available to you.

1. Who is responsible and how to contact us

Acorn Business Ventures Pvt Ltd operates BrickCircle.club in India. BrickCircle is a community-first service for adults to share access to sets through temporary exchanges, not a conventional buying-and-selling marketplace. “BrickCircle”, “we” and “us” refer to the service and its operator. For privacy questions, requests to exercise data rights, or a complaint about privacy, email support@brickcircle.club. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that a request concerns your account. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, authenticator keys, identity documents, or other highly sensitive information by ordinary email.

2. Information we handle

  • Account and profile: email address, authentication/provider identifier, display name, city and country, bio, profile image, adult-confirmation record, and account security information.
  • Collection and discovery: set numbers and catalogue details, owned-set condition and completeness, availability, wishlist items, exchange preferences, match and search activity, and photos you choose to upload of your assembled sets or profile.
  • Exchanges and communications: proposals, counterproposals, agreed durations and dates, handoff and return confirmations, exchange status and history, messages, reviews, reports, support requests, and administrative records needed to protect the service.
  • Device and service data: browser/device and network information, timestamps, diagnostic and security logs, session and authentication data, and service-worker/cache data needed for app functionality.
  • Notifications: if you enable browser push, a device-specific push subscription and notification delivery records. You can revoke browser notification permission in your device settings.
  • Analytics: page and feature use and limited event measures. Our app’s analytics instrumentation is designed to send enumerated events and aggregate dimensions, not account IDs, names, email addresses, free-text messages, or exchange IDs.

BrickCircle does not request precise GPS coordinates for matching. You provide your city and country. Google Analytics may also infer approximate location from technical signals such as network information, alongside browser and device information.

3. Why we use information

  • To create and secure accounts, authenticate members, and enforce the 18+ eligibility rule.
  • To maintain collections and wishlists, calculate local reciprocal matches, and show relevant set and member information.
  • To run the exchange workflow, preserve its history, send in-app, email, or enabled push notifications, and help members coordinate handoffs and returns.
  • To respond to support requests, investigate safety or abuse reports, protect members, and enforce our Terms of Use.
  • To troubleshoot, measure reliability, understand feature use, and improve the service.
  • To comply with applicable laws and protect or enforce legal rights.

Where a privacy law requires us to state a lawful basis, we rely as applicable on providing the service you request, our legitimate interests in security and service operation, your consent for processing that requires consent, and legal obligations. You can contact us to ask which basis applies to a particular use.

4. Information shown to other members

BrickCircle is a member-to-member community. Depending on the feature and your choices, signed-in members may see your display name, city and country, profile image, trust indicators, sets you list as available, relevant wishlist items, and information needed to consider or carry out an exchange. Messages and exchange details are shared with their participants. Do not put sensitive personal information in your profile, listing, or message. Do not publish a home address or private contact details unless you have independently decided they are necessary for an exchange.

5. Service providers and other disclosures

We use service providers to host and operate BrickCircle. These currently include Supabase for authentication, database, file storage, and server functions; Vercel for website hosting and delivery; Google for sign-in and Google Analytics; and Brevo for transactional email. Browser and operating-system push services may process notification requests if you enable them. Set catalogue images may be served by third-party image sources such as Brickset or an image proxy. Those providers may receive technical request data, such as your IP address and browser information, when your device requests their services.

We may also disclose information to other members as described above, to professional advisers bound by confidentiality, to a successor in a business transfer, or when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect safety or rights, investigate misuse, or enforce our agreements. We do not give members’ private messages to other members outside the conversation or a relevant safety/support process.

6. Cookies and analytics

BrickCircle uses essential storage for sign-in, security, preferences, and app functionality. Optional Google Analytics 4 is not loaded until you choose “Allow analytics” in the cookie notice. If you reject analytics, it remains off. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the site footer. The choice is stored in your browser. Analytics may use first-party cookies to distinguish visits or sessions and process page/feature events, browser/device information, and approximate location. Analytics is not loaded on the admin console or authentication callback page.

We use aggregated analytics to understand service use and improve the community. We configure event data to exclude account IDs, names, email addresses, free-text messages, and exchange IDs. Google may process information under its own privacy terms. You may also block or clear cookies through your browser, although this can affect sign-in and app functions.

7. Retention and account requests

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, maintain account and exchange records, support members, investigate abuse, protect security, comply with law, and resolve disputes. The period depends on the type of record and why it is held. Closing an account does not necessarily remove records needed to preserve another member’s exchange history, an open dispute, security evidence, or legal obligations. Where information is no longer needed, we will delete or de-identify it as reasonably practicable.

You may email us to request account closure, access, correction, deletion, or another data action. We will verify the request, consider applicable rights and exceptions, and explain if some information must be retained. We do not promise that every request can be completed where records are needed to protect another person or meet a legal obligation.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, obtain or transfer, restrict, or object to certain processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and raise a grievance with us or a relevant regulator. To exercise a right, contact support@brickcircle.club. We will respond under the time limits that apply to your request and jurisdiction. You may also be able to change browser notification permissions in your device settings.

9. International processing

BrickCircle is operated from India and may be used from other countries. Our providers may process or store information in countries different from yours. Where required by applicable law, we will use an available transfer mechanism or safeguard and provide relevant information on request.

10. Security and incidents

We use technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect account and exchange information, including authenticated access controls and database policies. No online service can guarantee perfect security. If we identify a personal-data incident that requires notice, we will notify affected people and authorities as required by applicable law.

11. Adults only

BrickCircle is intended only for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly allow a person under 18 to create an account or participate. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the report and take appropriate action.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. We will update the date above and provide additional notice for material changes where appropriate. The updated policy applies from its stated date, subject to any notice or consent required by law.
