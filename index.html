BrickCircle — the local LEGO exchange club for Adult Fans of LEGO

Catalogue your collectible LEGO sets, discover reciprocal matches with AFOL collectors in your city, meet safely in a public place, inspect both sets, and temporarily exchange them for 30, 60 or 90 days.

How BrickCircle works

Build your collection and wishlist. Match locally with another collector. Meet in person and inspect the sets together. Enjoy a temporary swap. Meet again to return and inspect the sets, then review your exchange experience and build your collector reputation.

BrickCircle beta supports local, in-person exchanges in selected major cities. Shipping is not part of the current beta.
