Experience · Exchange · Trade

One collection. A city of new LEGO experiences.

BrickCircle is a local club for Adult Fans of LEGO. Members temporarily swap collectible sets in person for 30, 60 or 90 days.

How a temporary swap works

01

Build your catalogue

Add the LEGO sets you own and mark the sets you are willing to exchange.

02

Create your wishlist

Choose sets you would like to experience without having to buy every set.

03

Find a reciprocal match

BrickCircle looks for a local collector who wants your set while owning a set you want.

04

Meet and inspect

Agree on 30, 60 or 90 days, meet in a public place, inspect both sets and confirm the handoff together.

05

Enjoy the temporary swap

Both physical sets remain reserved for the exchange until the return is completed.

06

Return and review

Meet again, inspect the returned sets, confirm the return and review the complete experience.

Beta model: local, in-person exchanges only in listed major cities. BrickCircle does not currently support shipping.
