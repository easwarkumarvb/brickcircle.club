Find your BrickCircle city.

BrickCircle beta is open to adult collectors in every city and country available in our registration selector. Exchanges and reciprocal matches remain local to members registered in the same city and country.

These are examples only. The registration selector contains the full set of cities currently available for the beta, and all of them participate in the same local matching system.

A city does not need a separate BrickCircle launch to participate. Matching begins whenever collectors in the same city and country create reciprocal LEGO interests.
