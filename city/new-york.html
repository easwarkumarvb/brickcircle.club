How BrickCircle New York works

Collection + wishlist

Add your collection, mark exchangeable sets and identify the LEGO sets you want to experience.

Reciprocal local matching

Look for the two-way match: a New York collector has something you want and wants something you own.

Public meetup

Arrange a public location and inspect both physical sets together before confirming the exchange.

30, 60 or 90 days

Choose a temporary swap duration. Both sets stay reserved until the exchange is returned or otherwise resolved.

Return inspection

Meet again at the end, inspect your own returned set and confirm satisfactory return.

Collector reputation

Completed exchanges and reviews can create a history that helps local collectors build trust over time.

Why exchange instead of buying every set?

Adult collectors can accumulate substantial Technic, Icons, architecture, space and display models that occupy both budget and storage. BrickCircle is designed to turn existing ownership into temporary access: two collectors can each experience a different set while retaining ownership of their original collection.

Local and face-to-face

The beta deliberately avoids shipping. Matching is city-based, and the workflow includes physical inspection at both handoff and return.
