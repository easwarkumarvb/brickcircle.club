A local LEGO exchange club for New York AFOLs.

Catalogue, match, meet, inspect and temporarily swap collectible LEGO sets with another adult collector in New York.

Local by design

The BrickCircle beta does not ship sets. Reciprocal matches are limited to collectors registered in the same city, with a second in-person meetup for the return.
