Paris · France

Your LEGO collection can become access to another collection.

BrickCircle helps Paris AFOLs discover reciprocal matches and temporarily exchange collectible sets while each collector keeps ownership of the original set.

Join Paris beta

From ownership to access

A collectible set may deliver its biggest experience during the original build and then spend much of its life displayed or stored. BrickCircle gives that existing set another role: it can help unlock a temporary experience with a set owned by another Paris collector.

Catalogue

Add your owned sets and decide which ones can participate in exchanges.

Wishlist

Choose sets you want to experience from another collector's collection.

Reciprocal match

Find a Paris member where both collectors have something meaningful to offer each other.

Physical inspection

Meet in a public place and inspect the sets together before the handoff.

Defined duration

Select a 30, 60 or 90-day temporary exchange with both sets reserved.

Return

Meet again, inspect your returned set, complete the exchange and review the experience.

A different way to experience collectible LEGO

For substantial Technic, Icons, architecture, space and display builds, reciprocal exchange can create variety without requiring every collector to permanently acquire every desired set.

Local and inspectable

The beta is designed around face-to-face exchanges rather than anonymous shipping. Both collectors see and inspect the physical sets at handoff and return.
