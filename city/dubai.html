Dubai · United Arab Emirates

High-value LEGO sets. A human, local exchange workflow.

BrickCircle helps Dubai AFOLs find reciprocal collection matches, meet face-to-face, inspect the physical sets and temporarily exchange them for a defined period.

Join Dubai beta

Designed around trust before handoff

Collectible LEGO sets can represent substantial money, time and personal attachment. BrickCircle's Dubai workflow therefore keeps the exchange local and makes physical inspection part of the process before either collector confirms a handoff.

Catalogue

Record the sets you own and identify which physical sets you are willing to exchange.

Wishlist

Select the sets you want to experience from another collector.

Reciprocal match

Look for another Dubai member where both collections can satisfy the other's wishlist.

Public meetup

Meet in a suitable public location and inspect both physical sets together.

Temporary handoff

Choose 30, 60 or 90 days. Both sets remain reserved throughout the active exchange.

Return and reputation

Meet again, inspect the returned sets, confirm completion and review the experience.

Experience more without permanently acquiring more

For collectors of Technic supercars, Icons, architecture, space and other substantial adult-oriented builds, reciprocal exchange offers another way to experience desirable sets while retaining ownership of an existing collection.

Local rather than anonymous

The beta does not rely on shipping. Direct handoff and return allow both collectors to inspect the actual sets and participate in the exchange lifecycle themselves.
