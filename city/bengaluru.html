How BrickCircle Bengaluru works

Add your collection and wishlist. BrickCircle looks for reciprocal matches among collectors registered in Bengaluru. If both members agree, choose a public meetup, inspect the sets, confirm the handoff, enjoy the temporary swap and meet again for the return.
