How BrickCircle Bengaluru works

Build your collection

Catalogue the LEGO sets you own, identify which ones you are willing to exchange and create a wishlist of sets you want to experience.

Find a Bengaluru match

The useful match is reciprocal: another collector in Bengaluru owns a set you want and wants an available set from your collection.

Meet and inspect

Agree on a public meetup and inspect both physical sets together before confirming the handoff.

Exchange temporarily

Choose a 30, 60 or 90-day swap. Both sets remain reserved throughout the active exchange.

Return together

Meet again at the end of the swap, inspect your returned sets and confirm the return before they become available again.

Build collector reputation

Completed exchanges can build a history of reliable participation within the BrickCircle community.

Why local exchanges?

Large Technic vehicles, Icons sets, architecture, space models and other substantial builds can spend years on display or in storage after the original build. A reciprocal exchange lets two Bengaluru collectors experience something new using sets they already own, without requiring either person to buy every set on a wishlist.

Safety is part of the workflow

The beta is intentionally local and does not rely on shipping. Members arrange a public meetup, inspect before handoff and meet again for the return. BrickCircle facilitates the workflow; members remain responsible for their personal safety, property and judgment.
