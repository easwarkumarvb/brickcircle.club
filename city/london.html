How a London LEGO swap works

Catalogue your sets

Add the sets you own, mark those available for exchange and create a wishlist.

Discover a reciprocal match

BrickCircle looks for another London collector who owns something you want and wants something you can offer.

Meet in public

Arrange a public meetup, inspect the sets together and confirm the handoff only when both collectors are satisfied.

Choose the duration

Temporary exchanges can run for 30, 60 or 90 days, with both physical sets reserved for the active swap.

Meet for the return

At the end of the period, meet again, inspect the returned sets and confirm completion.

Build reputation

Reviews of completed exchanges can help establish trusted collector histories over time.

Experience more from the collection you already own

For collectors of substantial Technic vehicles, Icons, architecture, space and other adult-oriented builds, buying every desired set can become expensive and storage-intensive. BrickCircle is designed around a different idea: ownership of one desirable set can become temporary access to another collector's set.

Local by design

The current beta does not ship sets. London exchanges use an in-person meetup before handoff and another meetup at return, allowing both collectors to inspect the physical sets themselves.
