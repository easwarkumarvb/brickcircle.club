Match within Tokyo

BrickCircle focuses on reciprocal local matches rather than one-sided requests or long-distance exchanges.

Inspect before swapping

Meet in a public place and check the physical sets together before confirming handoff.

Experience for a defined period

Choose 30, 60 or 90 days while both exchanged sets remain reserved.

Return and complete

Meet again, inspect the returned sets, confirm completion and review the experience.

Designed for collectors of substantial sets

BrickCircle can support exchanges across adult-oriented themes such as Technic, Icons, architecture, space and complex display builds. The model is especially useful when collectors want new building experiences without continually expanding permanent storage requirements.

A face-to-face beta

The current workflow does not depend on shipping. Physical inspection happens between the two collectors at handoff and again at return, keeping the condition of the actual set visible to both participants.
