A local LEGO exchange club for Singapore AFOLs.

Use your existing collection to experience another collector's sets through a reciprocal 30, 60 or 90-day swap.

Designed for face-to-face exchanges

Members arrange a public meetup, inspect before handoff and meet again at return. Active sets stay reserved throughout the temporary swap.
