Ownership becomes access

Collectible LEGO can be both rewarding and space-intensive. Technic vehicles, Icons models, architecture, space and other substantial builds may remain displayed or stored long after the initial build. BrickCircle lets two collectors use those existing collections to create another temporary experience.

No anonymous shipping in the beta

The current Singapore workflow is built around direct local handoff and return. This gives both collectors an opportunity to inspect the physical sets themselves at both ends of the exchange.
