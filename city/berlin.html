Reciprocal LEGO set exchanges for Berlin AFOLs.

BrickCircle helps adult LEGO collectors in Berlin turn sets already in their collections into temporary access to sets owned by another local enthusiast.

Join Berlin beta

Built around the two-way match

A useful BrickCircle match is more than finding someone who owns a desired set. The goal is reciprocity: a Berlin collector owns something you want to experience while also wanting an available set from your collection.

Catalogue

Add your collection and identify the physical sets you are prepared to exchange.

Wishlist

Choose substantial LEGO sets you would like to build, display or experience temporarily.

Local match

Discover compatible collectors registered in Berlin rather than arranging anonymous long-distance shipping.

Inspect together

Meet at a public place and inspect both sets before either collector confirms the handoff.

Temporary exchange

Select 30, 60 or 90 days. Both sets remain reserved during the active exchange.

Return and review

Meet again, inspect your returned set, complete the return and review the experience.

More LEGO experiences without a permanently larger collection

Adult-oriented Technic, Icons, architecture, space and display sets can require significant budget and storage. Reciprocal exchange offers another route: experience a different collector's set while retaining ownership of your own collection.

Physical inspection stays central

BrickCircle's beta is local and face-to-face. The workflow uses public meetups and inspection at both handoff and return instead of relying on shipping.
