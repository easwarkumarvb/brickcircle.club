The Sydney exchange lifecycle

Catalogue

Add the LEGO sets in your collection and mark which physical sets are available to exchange.

Wishlist

Choose the sets you would like to experience next.

Reciprocal matching

Find a Sydney collector whose available collection and wishlist create a useful two-way match.

Meet and inspect

Arrange a public meetup and examine both sets before confirming the temporary handoff.

30, 60 or 90 days

Both exchanged sets remain reserved while the temporary swap is active.

Return and reputation

Meet again, inspect the return, complete the exchange and review the experience.

Built for substantial collections

Many adult collectors eventually face the same trade-off: more desired builds versus more spending and storage. Reciprocal swapping can make an existing Technic, Icons, architecture, space or display collection useful in a new way by providing temporary access to another enthusiast's set.

Local rather than shipped

BrickCircle's beta keeps Sydney exchanges face-to-face. Direct inspection at handoff and return is part of the workflow rather than an optional extra.
