For Adult Fans of LEGO

Your collection can unlock someone else's collection.

BrickCircle turns ownership into access: catalogue the sets you own, discover reciprocal matches nearby and temporarily exchange sets with another adult collector.

Why an AFOL exchange club?

Many adult collectors own large, valuable sets that spend most of their lives displayed or stored after the build is complete. At the same time, another collector nearby may want to experience that exact set. BrickCircle is designed to connect those two interests without requiring either person to buy every set they want to experience.

Reciprocal matching

The useful match is not merely “someone owns it.” It is “they own what you want, and want what you own.”

Temporary, not permanent

Choose a 30, 60 or 90-day experience and then return the sets to their original owners.

Local community

Repeated in-person exchanges can create a network of collectors who know one another and build reputation over time.

What kinds of sets?

BrickCircle can support collectible sets across themes—from Technic supercars and complex vehicles to Icons, architecture, space and other substantial adult-oriented builds—provided both members agree to the exchange and inspect the physical sets together.
